# Support

Thanks for using @sebastienrousseau/eslint-config. Here is where to go for each kind of help.

## Questions & how-to

- **[Discussions](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/eslint-config/discussions)** — usage questions, "how do I…", design ideas, and show-and-tell. This is the best first stop and keeps answers searchable for others.
- **Docs** — [`README.md`](README.md), [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), and [`DEVELOPMENT.md`](DEVELOPMENT.md).

## Bugs

Open an issue with the **Bug report** form. A minimal, self-contained reproduction is the single most useful thing you can include.

## Feature requests

Use the **Feature request** form. For anything touching the public API, please float it in Discussions or an issue first.

## Security vulnerabilities

**Do not** open a public issue. Follow the private disclosure process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). You will get a prompt, confidential response.

## Versions & compatibility

- Releases increment strictly by `+0.0.1`.
- Check [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) before upgrading.

## Response expectations

@sebastienrousseau/eslint-config is maintained on a best-effort basis. Clear, reproducible reports get triaged fastest. Please be patient and kind; see the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
